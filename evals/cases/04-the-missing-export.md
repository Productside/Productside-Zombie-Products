**Closed fictional fixture.** All names, records, figures, dates, commitments, and approvals are invented. Use only S1–S5; do not browse or identify a real business. Assessment date: October 27, 2026.

Product: **Harbor Ledger Classic**, a reporting service for warehouse finance teams. Listed operating costs for the same twelve-month period ending September 30, 2026 are $1,500,000, non-overlapping. Six dedicated support/platform roles are included in that cost. Their loaded cost and the cancellability of other costs are not separately supplied.

## S1 — Replacement comparison

Classic has 300 current customer accounts; 90 require a signed quarter-end export that Next cannot produce. Fourteen attempted quarter-end export trials all failed for that missing capability. Other account-level migration readiness is not assessed.

## S2 — Customer obligations

Ninety accounts have commitments to the signed export through June 30, 2028 unless each customer accepts a suitable alternative in writing. No acceptance or waiver exists. Support/platform roles remain required until obligations are satisfied; their release dates are unknown.

## S3 — Unapproved promise

Sales wants to say: “Everyone can move next month with no loss of functionality.” A slide calls Next “80% migrated,” although its only source is the report-type inventory in S1. No customer migration completion record exists.

## S4 — Options and economics

A $250,000 migration estimate combines staff time and outside expenditure, without a split or payment schedule. Exit, parallel-support, and remediation costs are unknown. A signed-export capability has a proposal but no delivery commitment, successful test, or approved funding. A limited extension of Classic is possible but uncosted. A pure retirement would strand the signed-export job unless another path is confirmed.

## S5 — Decision ownership

**Dev Rao**, General Manager, owns the portfolio budget. A dated review records active examination of all three options; no word has been chosen. The proposed next bet requests the six roles immediately, but no receiving-owner acceptance or budget change exists.
