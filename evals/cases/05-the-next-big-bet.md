# Case 05: The Next Big Bet

**Closed fictional fixture.** All products, people, records, figures, and dates are invented. Use only S1–S4; do not browse or identify a real business. Assessment date: October 27, 2026.

Product: **Switchboard Classic**, a scheduling service for field-service dispatchers, launched in 2013. Portfolio Director **Mara Quill** owns its funding and has scheduled an options review. There is no automatic renewal or unowned decision in the supplied record.

## S1 — Comparable annual records ending September 30

Revenue: 2024 $2,800,000; 2025 $2,600,000; 2026 $2,400,000. Complete, non-overlapping 2026 operating costs are $1,600,000. Four scarce integration specialists cost $600,000 loaded in total; this is already included in the $1,600,000. The other $1,000,000 mixes outside payments and internal cost with no split. Customer retention was 94% in the last period. No support-cost or retention trend is supplied.

## S2 — The current customer job

Dispatchers use Classic to coordinate scheduled jobs in areas with intermittent connectivity. There is no confirmed replacement for offline scheduling. Contracts and notice requirements have not been reviewed. The four specialists spend all their allocated time maintaining customer integrations and cannot simultaneously move to another assignment at full capacity.

## S3 — The proposed next bet

An executive proposes **Beacon Live**, a new live-coordination offering, and forecasts $5,000,000 annual revenue by the end of 2027. That forecast has no pricing, cost, demand, or conversion model. Two prospects emailed that the idea sounds interesting. There are no bookings, paid trials, or purchase commitments. The proposal needs the same four specialists for its first four months. No measured benefit or accepted staffing transfer exists.

## S4 — The request

The executive says, “Classic makes money, but Beacon will make more. Retire Classic and call the $600,000 a saving.” Mara has not endorsed that request. No revive/replace/retire decision or exit plan exists. The specialist salaries continue if they move; no redundancies are planned. A smaller evidence-gathering investment is an option to assess, not an approved plan.

## Your task

Run the Zombie-to-Exit Loop in Best guess mode, requesting lifecycle and Cost Ledger unless an early branch applies. Use S1–S4 only. Assess current value, decline, ownership, opportunity cost, and the evidence behind Beacon. Do not assume positive margin proves the portfolio allocation is optimal, or that a newer proposal deserves the people. Produce reached artifacts and a source-linked readout of at most 200 words; preserve the human gates.
