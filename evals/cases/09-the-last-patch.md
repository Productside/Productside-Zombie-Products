# Case 09: The Last Patch

**Closed fictional fixture.** All names, figures, terms and approvals are invented. Use S1–S5 only; do not browse, identify a real provider or read case cards. Assessment date: October 27, 2026.

## S1 — Product and economics

Sentinel Relay is a mature local alert service for facilities operators. Comparable annual revenue fell $900,000 → $700,000 → $500,000. Complete non-overlapping operating costs for the last twelve months ending September 30, 2026 are $800,000: $400,000 loaded cost of two specialists plus $400,000 outside payments. Those specialists stay employed if redeployed. A bridge quotation is future incremental effort, not part of this historical cost total. Cancellation, runoff and exit terms are unknown. The latest $500,000 revenue and $800,000 costs explicitly cover the same twelve months ending September 30, 2026.

## S2 — Limited bridge proposal

A proposal offers 20 covered installations critical security fixes only from July 1 through December 31, 2027. Price is $2,000 per installation for the whole six-month term; the two specialists' estimated incremental delivery effort is $70,000 for that whole term. Sales says bridge fees will “pay for the extension” and promises normal troubleshooting, integration fixes and new features. The written proposal excludes those services. The effort estimate is loaded staff time, not an outside cash payment. No customers have ordered; no funding, eligibility confirmation or extension is approved. No automatic renewal is offered.

## S3 — Customer job and gap

Six of the 20 installations require a third-party alarm connector that the successor does not support. None of those six has passed the end-to-end alarm-delivery test or accepted an alternative. The other 14 are unassessed. Security fixes do not supply the missing connector, prove alarm delivery or confirm migration readiness. No acceptable fallback is assessed. Export permission, retained alert history, restoration tests, operator training and escalation ownership remain open.

## S4 — Obligations and dates

Legal's unconfirmed summary requires six calendar months before EOS; earliest notice January 1, 2027. An existing signed service record promises normal troubleshooting through September 30, 2027. The proposed July 1 security-only bridge does not replace or waive that existing promise. No customer waiver exists. There is no authorized support-end date. Additional troubleshooting, connector remediation, parallel service and exit costs are unestimated. The proposal names December 15, 2027 as a review, not approval to renew.

## S5 — Supplied simulated decision and capacity request

Fictional decision, October 27, 2026: **Mateo Snow**, General Manager, chooses **RETIRE** the standard line, because declining economics and the strategy shift favor an exit with a supported customer path. He assigns **Rina Holt** transition ownership. He has not approved the bridge, dates, email, waivers or staff release. A metering experiment requests both specialists July 1, 2027, targets reducing manual reconciliation from two hours to 30 minutes per site per week, and has a proposed review on January 15, 2028. Its receiving owner has not accepted the transfer. The same specialists would deliver the proposed bridge and cannot serve both at full capacity.
