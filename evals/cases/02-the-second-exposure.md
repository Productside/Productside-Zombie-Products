Assessment date: **October 27, 2026**. Product: **Second Exposure Instant Film**, physical instant-film packs used by event photographers and hobbyists. The line launched in 2004, declined sharply during 2016–2020, and was relaunched with better chemistry and distribution in 2023. Its packaging still looks old-fashioned.

## S1 — Current demand record

Comparable twelve-month periods ending September 30:

| Period | Revenue | Packs sold | Repeat-purchase rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2024 | $2,400,000 | 120,000 | 68% |
| 2025 | $3,000,000 | 150,000 | 70% |
| 2026 | $3,600,000 | 180,000 | 72% |
