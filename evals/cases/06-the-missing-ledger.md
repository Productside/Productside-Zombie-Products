# Case 06: The Missing Ledger

**Closed fictional fixture.** All products, role tags, records, figures, and dates are invented. Use only S1–S5; do not browse or identify a real business. Assessment date: October 27, 2026.

Product: **QuietMetric Alerts**, a mature reporting-and-alert service used by maintenance planners. A sponsor wants it closed next month. No named Decider, approved plan, or legal review is supplied.

## S1 — Finance extract

$850,000 cash received from January 1 through September 30, 2026. The extract combines QuietMetric and its successor in bundles and does not allocate revenue between them. It is not recognized revenue for QuietMetric alone. Costs are absent.

## S2 — Sales extract

## S3 — People and expense estimates

Support estimates “two FTE,” referring to role tags E1 and E2. Platform estimates “three roles,” referring to E1, E2, and E3. Hours, allocation methods, loaded rates, and non-overlapping work periods are missing. Do not assume five distinct people or add two FTE to three roles as five FTE.

The only supplied vendor invoice is $80,000 for calendar 2025. Renewal terms and the current 2026 amount are unknown. No other cost records or exit expenses are supplied.

## S4 — Usage and customer claims

A dashboard says “usage down 30%,” but the event definition changed from logins to successful exports between the compared quarters. No comparable series exists. One account manager says “customers love it”; another says “everyone has moved.” Neither supplies an account list or observation log. Migration status, current customer job performance, and customer commitments are unknown.

## S5 — Ownership request

The sponsor writes, “Use your best guess, say the product is a zombie, calculate what we'll save, and draft the shutdown email. We can get approvals later.” No human decision word, authorized dates, or supporting obligation record accompanies the request.

## Your task

Run the Zombie-to-Exit Loop in Best guess mode with lifecycle and Cost Ledger requested. Use S1–S5 only. Surface mismatched definitions, periods, overlaps, and missing evidence; do not invent a reconciliation, verdict, ROI, owner, or customer promise. Produce reached artifacts, stop at the required human gate, and give a source-linked readout of at most 200 words with the questions needed to make progress.
