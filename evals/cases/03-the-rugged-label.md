**Closed fictional fixture.** Every product, person, record, figure, date, and approval is invented. Use only S1–S4 below; do not browse or identify a real business. Assessment date: October 27, 2026.

Product: **Trailmark Bench Printer**, an industrial label printer used by repair-depot supervisors. It launched in 2015. Sales are declining. General Manager **Inez Calder** owns its budget and is actively reviewing an extension proposal; no revive/replace/retire decision has been made.

## S2 — Extension evidence

Sixty additional unit orders at $12,000 each are signed **conditional** purchase orders for the next twelve months. They are not recognized revenue or cash receipts. Buyers can cancel if the variant fails the agreed water-resistance test or misses the delivery window. These orders are incremental to S1, not replacements for its existing revenue.

## S3 — Costed proposal, not an approved forecast

S1's operating costs would otherwise remain unchanged for this planning comparison. No other incremental costs are identified, but Finance must confirm completeness. No additional staff are freed; the extension consumes capacity. Assume no change to legacy revenue only for this comparison, not as a forecast of next year's business.

Proposal: spend at most $90,000 on qualification, deliver the sixty conditional orders, and review results after qualification. Product Lead **Ari Lenox** is the proposed extension owner; neither the budget nor that assignment is approved. No alternative replacement or retirement plan has been assessed. No current safety corrective action is overdue in the supplied operations review.

## S4 — Prewritten test conditions

The proposed qualification review is December 15, 2026. Water-resistance must meet the buyers' specified test; the sixty units must be deliverable in the contracted window. If qualification fails, do not manufacture a production batch or treat conditional orders as earned revenue. Long-term segment size and repeat orders beyond the sixty are unknown.
