# Working with these digital takeaways

- Read `README.md` and `skills/README.md` before changing the published materials.
- Productside is a training and advisory firm. Describe these materials as digital takeaways and examples supporting its teaching and advisory services.
- Publish only the files explicitly allowed by `.gitignore`. Adding a new public file requires a deliberate change to that allowlist.
- Never add client names, real customer or learner data, credentials, private source material, or completed business plans.
- Use clearly labeled fictional worked examples. Keep Classic Reports consistent across the individual skills and templates. Real case cards in `case-studies/` require primary-source citations, checked dates, and separate facts, teaching interpretations and unknown outcomes; never attribute fictional exercise figures to the named real company.
- Preserve attribution, the existing license, evidence labels, and every human decision gate. An assistant must never confirm a decision or commitment on someone's behalf.
- Keep instructions, templates, and worked examples aligned. Check relative links and the staged file list before committing.
- Do not claim file checks prove that an assistant's behavior or a real customer outcome has been validated.
