# Worked example: Zombie-to-Exit Pack (cover and decision log)

**Fictional product, illustrative figures and dates.** The full artifacts are the worked examples in each step's `examples/sample.md`, which all follow Classic Reports.

## Cover

| | |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Classic Reports: desktop reporting portal for fleet operations managers, since 2014 |
| **Lifecycle stage** | Decline: revenue down three years, no new customers in 18 months |
| **Verdict** | Zombie at initial diagnosis; Morgan is appointed for the decision review |
| **The ledger, in one line** | What it earns above the line: about $1.1M from a shrinking base. What it eats below: two support agents, a third of a squad, an engine that loses support next year, and the module that would let its customers move on. |
| **Decision** | Replace with Insights (plus a fuel-tax report module) |
| **Decider** | Morgan Vale, fictional VP of Product |
| **Reason, simulated** | Close the fuel-tax gap and prove account migration before phasing out Classic in favor of Insights. |
| **Key dates** | NSC: Feb 1, 2027 · EOS: Aug 2, 2027 · EOL: Feb 1, 2028 · EOSL: May 1, 2028 |
| **The trade** | Proposed: assign only net releasable capacity after successor rollout, migration and Classic support. Amount/date and receiving acceptance Pending. Retained salaries are not cash savings. |

## Selected branch

Replace coordinates Insights rollout and old-product exit. Retain, Revive and Retire were compared in the Decision Record and are not selected. Retire would mean no successor. Dates below are proposals, not approved cutoffs or proof of readiness.
