## The four motions

### Retain (operate as is)
- **Current job:** fleet reporting, including twelve contracted customers.
- **As-is viability:** unproven; the engine loses vendor support next year and full allocation is incomplete.
- **Operating plan:** owner, budget/capacity ceiling, maintenance scope, review date and reconsideration triggers remain Pending. No invented indefinite funding.
- **Weakest point:** operation as is has unresolved cost and engine risk.

### Revive (extension play)
- **The play:** a "Classic Live" variant with dashboards for long-time customers.
- **Reasons that apply:** strengthen brand loyalty with a small segment.
- **Sunk-cost test:** would we start this today? No. Insights already does this better.
- **Weakest point:** rebuilds what Insights has, on an engine losing support.

### Replace (replacement play)
- **The replacement:** Insights, plus a fuel-tax report module.
- **Reasons that apply:** eliminate obsolete features; align with strategic goals; reduce cost.
- **Migration path:** move report types in two waves; fuel-tax reports ship in Insights before end of sale.
- **Weakest point:** depends on the fuel-tax module shipping on time.

### Retire (close without a successor)
- **Potential release:** net time and avoidable payments after closure work; no confirmed savings.
- **What it costs customers:** loss of fuel-tax reports with no successor in this plan. Insights would belong to the Replace branch.
- **Commitments to honor:** 12 multi-year contracts; six-month notice clauses *(illustrative)*.
- **Weakest point:** strands contracted customers without a path.

## Evidence

| | Customer evidence | Lifecycle signals | Business outcomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retain | current job; incomplete usage | weak as-is viability | full cost/funding incomplete |
| Revive | weak | weak | weak |
| Replace | strong (most customers already use Insights too) | strong | strong |
| Retire | weak (fuel-tax gap) | strong | strong |

## What must be true

| Move | For this to be right |
|---|---|
| Retain | Operating limits and funding approved, named owner, review date and measurable triggers, and engine risk addressed without an extension bet |
| Revive | A sizable segment would pay for Classic Live and would not move to Insights |
| Replace | Fuel-tax reports ship in Insights before end of sale; contracts allow migration |
| Retire | Closure is justified despite lost capability; contracts, notice, records and final support are honored without a successor promise |

## Stack-ranked recommendations

| Rank | Motion | Brief recommendation / strongest reason | Weakest point / condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Replace | Conditional: Insights already serves adjacent reporting jobs | Fuel-tax readiness, account acceptance, contracts, prices, data/support and rollout costs must be verified |
| 2 | Retain | Current reporting matters; assess operation as is within limits | No approved owner, ceiling or review; engine risk unresolved |
| 3 | Revive | Do not fund Classic Live on this evidence | Duplicates Insights; no verified incremental demand |
| 4 | Retire | Do not close without a successor on this evidence | The fuel-tax job and twelve contracts remain |

These are recommendations for this fictional evidence, not a universal ranking or a human decision.

## The call

**Word:** Replace

**Said by:** Morgan Vale, fictional VP of Product

**Date:** October 28, 2026 (simulated)

**Reason, in their words:** "Insights is where the customers already are. We close the fuel-tax gap first, then we move everyone, on a calendar they can see."

**Weighed differently:** gave more weight to the 12 contracts than the team did; made the fuel-tax module a precondition for end of sale.
