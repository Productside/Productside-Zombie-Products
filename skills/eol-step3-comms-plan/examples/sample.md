## 4. Customer path

- **Affected:** all remaining Classic Reports accounts, from usage data; 12 contracted accounts flagged.
- **They move to:** Insights, with the fuel-tax module.
- **Cost to them:** proposed first-year discount; exact price, renewal, exclusions, taxes and eligibility Unknown. No universal free move.
- **Support continues:** full support to EOL, limited support to May 1, 2028.
- **Key messages (draft, gated):** (1) Insights is the proposed successor, subject to fuel-tax readiness and account acceptance. (2) Dates and support must be confirmed against your contract. (3) Casey Reed coordinates a tested path; unresolved accounts are not forced out by a generic cutoff.

## 6. Rules of the road

Actions remain planned. Fuel-tax readiness, account acceptance, permissions, rollback and contracts gate exit. If readiness slips, review the schedule and preserve obligations; do not claim every customer has a safe migration.

## 7. Open risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Fuel-tax module slips | End of sale moves with it; communicate the new date at once |
| A contracted account refuses to move | Legal reviews options; paid extended support where strategic |
| Sales keeps bundling after EOS | Systems block the bundle SKU on Aug 2, 2027 |
