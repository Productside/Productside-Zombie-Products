# Worked example: EOL Action Planning Worksheet

**Fictional product, illustrative dates.** Continues from the Decision Record example.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Product** | Classic Reports |
| **Decision** | Replace with Insights (plus a fuel-tax report module) |
| **Plan owner** | The newly named exit product manager |
| **Product type** | Digital |

## 1. Phase timeline

| Phase | Our date | Note |
|---|---|---|
| NSC · Notice of Status Change | Feb 1, 2027 | Six-month lead time to match contract notice clauses |
| EOS · End of Sale | Aug 2, 2027 | Only if the fuel-tax module has shipped in Insights |
| EOE · End of Expansion | Nov 1, 2027 | No new report templates or seats |
| EOL · End of Life | Feb 1, 2028 | Support transition begins |
| EOSL · End of Service Life | May 1, 2028 | Final day of support; data export available until then |

|---|---|---|---|

| 9 | Partners | Jan 26, 2027 | Their customer list and dates |
| 10 | Customers | Feb 1, 2027 | The calendar, the path to Insights, support until May 1, 2028 |

## 4. Customer path

- **Affected:** all remaining Classic Reports accounts, from usage data; 12 contracted accounts flagged.
- **They move to:** Insights, with the fuel-tax module.
- **Cost to them:** migration discount for the first year *(illustrative)*.
- **Support continues:** full support to EOL, limited support to May 1, 2028.
- **Key messages:** (1) Your reports move to Insights, including fuel tax. (2) Here are the dates; nothing changes before August. (3) Your named contact will walk you through it.

## 5. Key action items

| Action | Phase | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Announce the replacement product | NSC | Product marketing |
| Compare old and new features | NSC | Product enablement |
| Engage distributors and channels | NSC | Partner management |
| Revise sales forecasts | NSC | Finance |
| Plan old versus new licensing | NSC | Licensing |
| Update marketing materials | NSC | Product marketing |
| Capture churn versus conversions | EOS onward | Customer success |
| Modify sales and service systems | EOS | IT and systems |

## 6. Rules of the road

All seven "do" items have a line in the plan. No "do not" is violated. Watch item: end of sale is gated on the fuel-tax module; if it slips, end of sale slips, not the customers.

## 7. Open risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Fuel-tax module slips | End of sale moves with it; communicate the new date at once |
| A contracted account refuses to move | Legal reviews options; paid extended support where strategic |
| Sales keeps bundling after EOS | Systems block the bundle SKU on Aug 2, 2027 |
