# Retirement Closure Worksheet

**Decision: Retire — close the product without providing a successor.** Complete only after a compatible named human decision. Records/export access, refunds and temporary support are closure measures, not a successor. If a product is to take over the job, expose the conflict and ask the Decider; use [Replacement Launch and Exit](replace-template.md) only after clarification.

- **Closure conditions:** verified commitments, unresolved cohorts, payments, records and final support. No automatic waiver, deletion or forced cutoff from a generic date.
- **Continuing controls and other products:** explicitly outside this retirement where still required.
- **Customer job:** explain what will no longer be provided. No successor or successful migration claim. Unresolved needs are impact/risk to address, not evidence for inventing a replacement.
- **Economics:** current cost, revenue runoff, continuing support, refunds, records and closure costs separately. Potential release is not confirmed cash or accepted staff transfer.
- **Key messages (draft, gated):** capability ending and reason; verified notice/access/support; records and payment assistance; named contact. Do not promise a new product or indefinite support.
- **Key actions:** closure notice; account/commitment review; refunds and failed-payment remedy where applicable; stop fresh promises through approved Sales/catalog controls; records/access/support plan; forecasts and licensing; materials; customer-impact monitoring; final decommissioning only when authorized.
