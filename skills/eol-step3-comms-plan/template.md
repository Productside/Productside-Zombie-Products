# Step 3 worksheet selector

Use the human's recorded motion and product boundary. Read and complete **one** worksheet:

| Decision | Worksheet | Required distinction |
|---|---|---|
| **Replace** | [Replacement Launch and Exit](replace-template.md) | Successor rollout plus old-product exit; coverage, migration, acceptance and overlap |
| **Retire** | [Retirement Closure](retire-template.md) | Closure without a successor; capability loss, obligations, records and final support |

Both include ordered conversations: who hears, why, from whom, by when, and the next action/owner. Preserve every approval status.

**Retain** and **Revive** use their Operating or Extension Plan in the [Decision Record](../eol-step2-revive-or-replace/template.md), not an EOL worksheet. If the recorded motion conflicts with the proposed successor, stop for the Decider to clarify; never silently relabel it.

The [Markdown takeaway](../../templates/EOL-Action-Planning-Worksheet.md) includes the four-motion decision and Operating/Extension plans; the [Excel takeaway](../../templates/EOL-Action-Planning-Worksheet.xlsx) also provides separate Replace and Retire tabs and a fictional Replace example. Use the matching worksheet for the recorded motion; historical evaluation reports do not establish validation of the new branches.
