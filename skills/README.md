# The zombie-to-exit skills

Seven skills help you examine one aging product, decide what should happen, plan the transition, and redirect the freed capacity. Six cover individual steps; the seventh runs them together. These are digital takeaways and examples supporting Productside's teaching and advisory services.

## The sequence

Lifecycle map → Zombie ID → Cost Ledger → Revive/Replace/Retire → Exit plan → Announcement → Capacity Transfer.

The lifecycle map is optional when the stage is clear. The Cost Ledger is required for a capacity transfer. A healthy or merely dated product stops the exit loop before the ledger and later steps; a standalone ledger can be requested separately. A revive decision follows a different path from retirement. The loop explains these branches.

At each hard stop and at the end, the loop leads with a readout of at most 200 words, with source references, decision status, risks, and the next action. The reached artifacts follow separately.

## How to use a skill

Open its `SKILL.md` and supply the instructions to your AI assistant, together with the linked template and companion skills it needs. Each skill folder includes:

- `SKILL.md`: instructions and human decision gates.
- `template.md`: the output structure, also usable by hand.
- `examples/sample.md`: a worked example following **Classic Reports**, a fictional product with illustrative figures and dates.

Choose **Guided** for questions one at a time, **Context dump** to work from existing notes, or **Best guess** to draft with assumptions labeled. Best guess never authorizes the assistant to make the revive, replace, or retire decision.

For local Claude Code use and a starter prompt, see the [Project README](../README.md#use-the-skills).

## Use the worksheet directly

The [Markdown worksheet](../templates/EOL-Action-Planning-Worksheet.md) and [Excel worksheet](../templates/EOL-Action-Planning-Worksheet.xlsx) can be used without an assistant. Excel includes a blank worksheet and a fictional worked example; that example is a starting point, not a completed plan for your product.

A retirement can offer customers a similar product from another vendor. Coordinate the integration, data and support handoff, eligibility, discounted price, renewal terms, and any new bill for bundled users before making promises.

Timing intervals are planning defaults. Confirm actual obligations with the responsible legal, finance, and plan owners before making customer promises.
