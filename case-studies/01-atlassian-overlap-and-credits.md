# Atlassian: give customers room to move

**Record:** current migration-program guidance. **Checked:** October 27, 2026. Program mechanisms; completed retirement outcomes are unverified.

## What the public record establishes

Atlassian documents migration trials for eligible Data Center customers, linked to their existing subscription period. It describes annual-subscription step-up credits for remaining subscription value, and dual licensing that extends the old license alongside an annual Cloud purchase so customers can operate both during testing or phased migration. Trial limitations include no financially backed SLA and no trial data backup. Eligibility and exclusions matter. [Atlassian migration guidance](https://support.atlassian.com/migration/docs/cloud-migration-trials-for-server-and-data-center-customers/).

## What this teaches — our interpretation

Budget for a period in which customers can check the destination and move safely. Treat unused-value credits, trial access and overlap as separate commercial mechanisms; establish whether they combine. Confirm backup and support arrangements for the transition.

## What the evidence cannot establish

This guidance does not establish any specific customer's savings, successful migration, satisfaction or retained relationship. It does not supply Atlassian's product-level margins or capacity redeployment. It does not show that all trials carry production protections.

## Practice the decision

[The Overlap Window](../evals/cases/08-the-overlap-window.md) uses invented terms and economics. It tests credits, overlap cost, readiness, support and staffing timing. Its terms are not Atlassian's offer.
