# Netflix DVD: close the service and honor what customers valued

**Record:** closure announcement, April 18, 2023; final-shipment confirmation, September 29, 2023. **Sources checked:** October 28, 2026.

## What the public record establishes

Netflix announced that DVD.com would close later in 2023, explaining that a shrinking business would make maintaining service increasingly difficult. It named September 29 as the final shipment date and thanked members and employees for the service's 25-year history. [Netflix's closure announcement](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/netflix-dvd-the-final-season).

On September 29, Netflix confirmed it had shipped its last DVDs that morning. Its farewell celebrated the catalog, customer choice and the experience of receiving the red envelopes. This establishes an announced milestone followed by a reported completion, not just an intention to close. [Netflix's final-shipment confirmation](https://about.netflix.com/en/news/thanks-for-watching).

## What this teaches — our interpretation

For the DVD-by-mail service, this is a **Retire teaching pattern**: closure of a specific service. Netflix's streaming business should not automatically be treated as an equivalent successor for every DVD customer's job. Check catalog coverage, physical access and customer acceptance before proposing any Replace path.

Explain the reason and the milestone plainly; acknowledge what people valued. Sequence internal alignment, customer notice, remaining service and final confirmation. A respectful farewell can be part of the plan, but it cannot substitute for resolving obligations and customers' remaining needs.

## What the evidence cannot establish

These announcements do not establish DVD-level profitability, a quantified capacity transfer, customer retention in streaming, satisfaction or an equivalent destination for every title and customer. They do not document every account's billing, returns or resolution. A shrinking business alone does not prove that it was an ownerless zombie; neither announcement supplies a Retain decision record for earlier years.
