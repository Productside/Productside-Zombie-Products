# Hipchat and Stride: a customer destination outside the portfolio

**Record:** partnership announcement, July 26, 2018. **Source checked:** October 27, 2026.

## What the public record establishes

Atlassian announced that it would discontinue Stride and Hipchat offerings and work with Slack on migration paths for customers of the four named products. Slack acquired intellectual property for Stride and Hipchat Cloud; Atlassian made an equity investment in Slack. The announcement described deeper integrations with remaining Atlassian products and a sharper focus for Atlassian. [Atlassian's announcement](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/development/new-atlassian-slack-partnership).

## What this teaches — our interpretation

An external destination can preserve a customer's job while the originating provider exits a product category. Assign ownership across providers for migration, integrations, terms and escalation. Distinguish a partnership announcement from account-level readiness and acceptance.

## What the evidence cannot establish

The announcement does not establish universal migration success, a discount for every customer, retained relationships, product-level profitability or a particular staffing transfer. It does not establish the economics of any private practitioner example.
