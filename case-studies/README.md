# Real exits, usable lessons

Four public records show ways to retire a product while helping customers continue their work. These case cards support Productside's teaching and advisory services. They establish documented actions, announcements or program terms—not that the products were zombies or that every customer relationship survived.

Read a card to learn the pattern. For evaluation, give the assistant **only the fictional exercise and skill instructions**, withholding these cards, evaluator criteria, worked examples and prior answers. The fictional exercises introduce invented economics, owners, approvals and constraints so a decision can be tested without attributing private knowledge to a real company.

Each card separates the public record, teaching interpretation, and what the evidence cannot establish. Historical announcements are dated; current program guidance was checked October 27, 2026 and can change. Check the linked provider's current terms before using a commercial offer.

Use the [Customer Transition Scorecard](../templates/Customer-Transition-Scorecard.md) to work through customer jobs, costs, data/integrations/support, blocked accounts, and the capacity trade. The [evaluation guide](../evals/README.md) explains how actual responses are graded.

## Evidence rules

- Cite a primary source beside each material factual statement. Date an announcement; use an access date for undated guidance.
- Describe an offer as offered or announced until completion is documented.
- Report measured outcomes only when a source supplies them. Unpublished margin, approvals, retention and capacity transfer remain unknown.
- Keep teaching suggestions labeled as interpretation. Do not copy invented figures or decisions into the factual card.
- Do not add private client stories or source materials. A recognizable company name belongs here only as a sourced public example.
