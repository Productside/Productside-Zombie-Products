# Microsoft: a bridge with a defined scope

**Record:** current lifecycle-program guidance. **Checked:** October 27, 2026. A support mechanism; customer outcomes are unverified.

## What the public record establishes

Microsoft describes Extended Security Updates as a temporary option for eligible legacy products while customers migrate. Coverage is for specified security updates; it does not extend the normal product lifecycle or restore standard technical support. New features, customer-requested non-security updates and design changes are excluded. Terms vary by product and deployment. [Microsoft ESU FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/lifecycle/faq/extended-security-updates).

## What this teaches — our interpretation

A limited bridge can help a blocked customer without reopening the entire product roadmap. Define covered systems, exclusions, owner, cost, migration milestones and a review date. Explicitly account for needs the bridge cannot cover.

## What the evidence cannot establish

The policy does not prove that a particular customer can migrate, is safe from every threat, or receives full support. It supplies no general rule that an extension is profitable or the best allocation. Current eligibility cannot be inferred from a historical example.
