# How to Kill Zombie Products

## Your Zombie Survival Kit

Keeping a product alive after it stops creating enough value can cost more than retiring it. These digital takeaways help you recognize the problem, make an evidence-backed decision, protect the customer relationship, and put the freed capacity to work.

From Productside's **Post Launch Success Series (Part 1): How to Kill Zombie Products**, October 28, 2026, with Dean Peters and Kenny Kranseler.

## Use the skills

You do not need to install anything to read or use the worksheets. To use a skill with an AI assistant:

1. Open its `SKILL.md` link above or in the [skill guide](skills/README.md).
2. Copy the instructions into your assistant. Provide the linked template and any companion skills when the instructions call for them.
3. Describe the product and the evidence you have. Choose guided questions, a context dump, or a best guess with assumptions labeled.
4. Review every result. A human owns the decision, figures, dates, customer promises, and capacity commitments.

Start with public or fictional information. Use an organization-approved environment for confidential product or customer information. Keep completed plans outside this public Project.

For local Claude Code use, clone or download this Project and open that folder in Claude Code. The Project includes a `.claude/skills` link to the skill folders. If your download or system does not preserve that link, use the copy-and-paste route above. Example invocation:

```text
/eol-run-zombie-to-exit
Classic Reports is a fictional reporting portal for fleet operations managers.
Revenue has fallen for three years and support load is rising.
Start with guided questions; do not invent missing figures.
```

The announcement step can use Dean Peters' separate [eol-message skill](https://github.com/deanpeters/Product-Manager-Skills/tree/main/skills/eol-message). It is not bundled here. The loop includes a fallback if that skill is unavailable.

## Five rules to take back to your team

1. **Recognize the Walking Dead.** Count the full cost of keeping a product alive.
2. **Decide: Revive, Replace, or Retire.** Use evidence, not nostalgia.
3. **Kill the Product, Not the Customer Relationship.** Give customers a path and clear communication.
4. **Make Retirement a Team Sport.** Bring legal and finance in early; align everyone who carries a commitment.
5. **Free the Living from the Dead.** Name where the freed capacity goes and who owns the next outcome.

## Examples and limits

All worked examples follow **Classic Reports**, a fictional product. Names, figures, dates, and decisions in those examples are illustrative. They are not customer evidence or a plan to adopt unchanged.

AI outputs are drafts. Verify facts and calculations, and have the responsible people review commitments before acting. These materials support product judgment; they do not replace it.
